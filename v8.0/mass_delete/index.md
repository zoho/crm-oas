# Mass Delete Records API

- [OpenAPI specification](mass_delete.yaml)
- [Delete records in bulk by record IDs or Custom View](operations/massDelete/operation.md)
  - To delete records in bulk from a specified module in your Zoho CRM organization, either by providing record IDs for synchronous deletion or a Custom View ID for asynchronous batch deletion.
  - [Examples](operations/massDelete/examples/)
- [Retrieve mass delete job status](operations/getMassDeleteJobStatus/operation.md)
  - To retrieve the status and record counts of a previously scheduled mass delete job in your Zoho CRM organization.
  - [Examples](operations/getMassDeleteJobStatus/examples/)
