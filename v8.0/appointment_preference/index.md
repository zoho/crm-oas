# Appointment Preferences Apis

- [OpenAPI specification](appointment_preference.yaml)
- [Get Appointment Preferences](operations/getAppointmentPreference/operation.md)
  - Fetches the existing configuration for appointment preferences, including rules for deal creation, job sheet visibility, and mark as complete configurations.
  - [Examples](operations/getAppointmentPreference/examples/)
- [Update Appointment Preferences](operations/updateAppointmentPreference/operation.md)
  - To update the appointment preference settings in your Zoho CRM organization, including booking constraints, job sheet requirements, deal creation configuration, and record-sharing settings.
  - [Examples](operations/updateAppointmentPreference/examples/)
