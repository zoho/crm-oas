# Inventory Templates

- [OpenAPI specification](inventory_templates.yaml)
- [Inventory templates](mds/getInventoryTemplates.md)
  - To retrieve a paginated list of inventory templates configured in your Zoho CRM organization, with optional filtering by module, category, and field criteria, and sorting by name or usage and modification timestamps.
  - [Examples](mds/examples/getInventoryTemplates.md)
- [Inventory Template](mds/getInventoryTemplateById.md)
  - Retrieves a single inventory template by its ID with complete details including content and all metadata.
  - [Examples](mds/examples/getInventoryTemplateById.md)
