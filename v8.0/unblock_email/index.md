# Unblock Email Address API

- [OpenAPI specification](unblock_email.yaml)
- [Unblock emails for multiple records in a module](operations/unblockEmailByModule/operation.md)
  - To unblock soft-bounced email addresses for multiple records in your Zoho CRM organization. When emails sent from CRM bounce temporarily, the Email field on the affected records is blocked. This operation removes those blocks in bulk so email communication with the records can resume.
  - [Examples](operations/unblockEmailByModule/examples/)
- [Unblock email for a single record](operations/unblockEmailById/operation.md)
  - To unblock a soft-bounced email address for a single record in your Zoho CRM organization. When an email sent from CRM bounces temporarily, the Email field on the affected record is blocked. This operation removes that block so email communication with the record can resume.
  - [Examples](operations/unblockEmailById/examples/)
